Short, practical moves you can use today. Each one links to the full reference.

## Learn three shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd> | Inspect Mode on or off. Works while a menu or dialog is open. |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> | Sidebar open or closed. |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> | Show or hide the dock. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Closes the innermost layer and returns focus to where you were. |

In the note form, <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> adds a line. To change a shortcut, open this address in Chrome:

```text
chrome://extensions/shortcuts
```

See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Keep the sidebar out of the way

The sidebar opens as a **compact rail**: a slim column of numbered dots that expands when you hover it. Click the rail icon in the sidebar header to switch between the rail and the full panel. Bluelines remembers your choice. To set what it opens as, use **Settings → Workspace → Sidebar opens as**: **Compact rail** or **Full panel**. See [Compact rail](/docs/sidebar-and-status#compact-rail-or-full-panel).

## Rename a category everywhere

Double-click the category name on the chip in the note form, type the new name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Or open the chip's arrow and click **Rename “…”** at the bottom of the menu. Every saved note in that category, on every page, moves to the new name. The toast says, for example, "Renamed Copy to Content". You can do the same in **Settings → Annotations → Your categories** by editing a name. See [Rename a category](/docs/notes-and-categories#rename-a-category-everywhere).

> **Tip:** Deleting a category in Settings doesn't delete its notes. They keep the old name.

## Paste a screenshot into Figma or Slack

**Copy image** on a card copies only the screenshot, as a picture. Paste it straight into Figma, Slack or a doc. The card then shows a green **Copied** badge that stays, so you can see what you've already shared. Click a thumbnail to see the screenshot full size first.

## Set what Copy copies

The copy button in the sidebar header shows the current format, **JSON** by default. Click it to choose **Markdown**, **XML**, **Plain text**, or any app turned on in **Settings → Share to apps** (Notion, Linear and Jira are on by default). Every card's **Copy** button then uses that format. Picking a format from the table's **Copy as** menu sets it too. See [Copy one note](/docs/export-and-share#copy-one-note).

## Make panels readable on dark or busy sites

**Settings → Appearance → In-page panels** sets how Bluelines' panels look over the page:

- **Frosted glass**: the default, a blurred panel.
- **Glass** and **Liquid glass**: more of the page shows through. Liquid glass is the most transparent and the hardest to read on pure white or pure black pages.
- **Solid**: hides the page behind the panel completely, and is lighter on slower machines.

On a very dark or very bright site, pick **Solid**. Set **Theme** to **Dark**, **Light** or **System** to match. See [Appearance](/docs/appearance#in-page-panels).

## Colour notes by category

**Settings → Workspace → Note colours → By category** gives each category its own colour on card numbers and page pins. **Accent** uses your accent colour for all of them. See [Workspace](/docs/workspace#note-colours).

## Hide the dock, keep the shortcuts

Click the **×** on the dock to hide it. The toast says how to get it back: press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>, or use the toolbar icon and **Open sidebar**. Inspect Mode and the sidebar shortcuts keep working while it's hidden.

To move the dock instead, drag it by its handle. It snaps to the nearest edge. From the keyboard: focus the handle, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, use the arrow keys (<kbd>Shift</kbd> for bigger steps), and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> again.

## Use the pins

While the sidebar is open, numbered pins mark each noted element on the page. The numbers match the cards. Click a pin to jump to its card. Click a card to scroll to its element and pulse it. If pins get in the way of a screenshot or a demo, set **Settings → Workspace → Page pins → Hide**. See [Page pins](/docs/sidebar-and-status#page-pins).

## Fit the table to your screen

Drag a column edge in the table to resize it. Bluelines remembers the widths. Double-click the edge to reset that column ("Column width reset").

## Turn motion down

**Settings → Appearance → Motion → Reduced** turns off animations, the trailing Inspect ring and smooth scrolling. **Follow system** uses your operating system's setting.
