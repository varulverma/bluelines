You need Bluelines installed and a normal web page open. If you haven't installed it yet, see [Install](/docs/install).

## Your first note in 60 seconds

1. **Open a page you want to review.** Any `http`, `https` or `localhost` page works. The Bluelines dock floats at the edge of the page.
2. **Turn on Inspect Mode.** Click **Inspect** in the dock, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>.
3. **Hover the page.** Each element under your pointer gets an outline, a selector chip above it and a size chip (for example `320 × 48 px`) below it.
4. **Click the element you want to comment on.** Inspect Mode turns off, the element stays outlined, and a note callout opens beside it, joined by a connector line.
5. **Write your note** in the field that says *What should change here?* Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line.
6. **Save.** Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click the arrow button. A message says **Annotation saved**, and the count badge on the dock goes up.
7. **See it in the sidebar.** Click the speech-bubble button in the dock (**Annotations sidebar**), or press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd>. Your note is a numbered card, and a matching numbered pin marks the element on the page.
8. **Jump back to it.** Click the card. The page scrolls to the element and it pulses.

That's it. Your note is saved in your browser and stays with this page's address.

> **Tip:** To leave a note that isn't about one element, open the sidebar and click **+** in its header. That adds a general note with no location.

> **Note:** Clicking outside the note callout, or pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd>, closes it without saving.
